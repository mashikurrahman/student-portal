# Study in Germany — Complete Guide for Bangladeshi Students

**Last researched:** 2026-07-25
**Audience:** Bangladeshi students applying for Bachelor's / Master's in Germany.

> ⚠️ Figures (fees, blocked-account amount, deadlines) change yearly and per
> program. Every number here is **indicative** — always confirm on the official
> program page, uni-assist, DAAD, and the German Embassy Dhaka before applying.

---

## 1. Why Germany

- **Little or no tuition.** Most **public** universities charge **no tuition fees**,
  even for international students — you pay only a **semester contribution of ~€100–€400**
  (covers administration, student services, and usually a public-transport "semester ticket").
- **2,000+ English-taught programs**, especially at Master's level (STEM, data science,
  business, sustainability).
- **Strong Bangladeshi community** — Bangladesh is ~16th worldwide for students in Germany
  (~6,600 enrolled), concentrated in Berlin, Aachen, and other engineering hubs.
- **Post-study work:** 18-month residence permit to look for a job after graduation.

**Important exception:** **TU Munich (TUM)** now charges non-EU international students
tuition (~€4,000–€6,000 per semester for Master's, ~€2,000–€3,000 for Bachelor's) from
winter 2024/25 onward. Baden-Württemberg state universities also charge non-EU students
~€1,500/semester. Most other public universities remain tuition-free.

## 2. The Bangladesh-specific facts (read this first)

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **APS certificate** | ❌ **NOT required for Bangladesh.** Unlike India, Pakistan, China, Vietnam, and Mongolia, Bangladeshi students do **not** need APS academic verification. There is no APS office for Bangladesh. (Saves ~4 weeks and ~US$250–300.) |
| **Blocked account** | ✅ Required for the visa. **~€11,904 for one year (~€992/month)** for 2025–26, based on the BAföG rate. Providers: Fintiba, Expatrio, Coracle, Deutsche Bank. |
| **Visa fee** | €75 (≈ paid in BDT at the embassy on interview day; €37.50 for minors). |
| **Documents** | Bangladesh applicants submit **2 complete sets** of documents at the embassy. |
| **Appointment wait** | Visa-appointment waiting times at the German Embassy Dhaka can be **long (often many months)** — register for an appointment as early as possible. |
| **Embassy** | German Embassy Dhaka — the authoritative source. Register online for the appointment before submitting. |

> Some India-focused blogs wrongly list Bangladesh as APS-mandatory. The German
> Embassy Dhaka is the authority — and it confirms **no APS for Bangladesh**. Verify
> at the embassy site before you rely on any third-party claim.

## 3. Entry requirements (typical)

### Academic
- **Bachelor's programs:** completed HSC (12 years of schooling). Many Bangladeshi
  students need **1–2 semesters of prior university study** or a **Studienkolleg**
  (foundation year) + assessment exam (Feststellungsprüfung) to match the German
  "Abitur" standard.
- **Master's programs:** a relevant **Bachelor's degree**. Competitive programs
  typically want a **good GPA — roughly ≥ 3.0/4.0 (≈ 60%+ / German grade ≤ 2.5)**;
  top programs expect higher.

### Language
- **English-taught programs:** **IELTS 6.5** (usually min 6.0 per band) or **TOEFL iBT ~85–90**.
  Some quantitative programs ask for IELTS 7.0.
- **German-taught programs:** **C1 German** — TestDaF 4×4 (TDN 4), DSH-2, or Goethe C1.
- Even for English programs, **A1–B1 German** helps with daily life, part-time jobs, and the visa.

### Sometimes required
- **GRE** (some technical Master's), **GMAT** (some business schools).
- **CV, Statement of Purpose (motivation letter), 1–2 recommendation letters.**

## 5. How to apply — step by step

1. **Pick programs** (this portal) and read each program's official requirements page.
2. **Sit language/standardized tests** (IELTS/TOEFL; GRE/GMAT if needed). Book early.
3. **Prepare documents** (see checklist below); get **certified English translations**
   of anything not in English/German (court-certified translator).
4. **Apply** — either the **university's own portal** or **uni-assist** (a central
   service that evaluates international applications for ~180 universities and forwards
   them). Some universities require uni-assist; some don't.
5. **Watch deadlines & processing time** — uni-assist needs **4–6 weeks**, so submit
   **weeks before** the university deadline (submit in **May/early June** for a July 15
   winter deadline).
6. **Receive admission** (conditional/unconditional).
7. **Open a blocked account** and get **health insurance**.
8. **Register the visa appointment** at the German Embassy Dhaka and prepare **2 document sets**.
9. **Attend the visa interview** (biometrics + interview), pay €75.
10. **Fly, enroll (matriculate), register your address (Anmeldung), get a residence permit.**

### Application deadlines (general)
| Intake | Starts | Typical deadline |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Winter semester** (most intake) | October | **July 15** (many Master's/competitive courses are earlier — March–June) |
| **Summer semester** (fewer programs) | April | **January 15** |

## 6. Document checklist

**Core application set (to apply):**
- [ ] Academic transcripts (Bachelor's/HSC) + **certified English translation**
- [ ] Degree certificate / diploma (or provisional)
- [ ] English proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL) — or German C1 for German programs
- [ ] CV / résumé
- [ ] Statement of Purpose (motivation letter)
- [ ] 1–2 letters of recommendation
- [ ] Passport (bio page)
- [ ] Passport photos
- [ ] (If required) GRE/GMAT scorecard
- [ ] (If required) uni-assist **VPD** (Vorprüfungsdokumentation)

**Post-admission / visa set:**
- [ ] University admission letter (Zulassung)
- [ ] Blocked account confirmation (~€11,904)
- [ ] Health insurance confirmation
- [ ] Visa application forms (2 sets) + declaration
- [ ] Proof of accommodation (if available)
- [ ] Biometric photos for visa

## 7. Scholarships & funding

| Scholarship | Who / what | Amount |
|-------------|-----------|--------|
| **DAAD EPOS** (Development-Related Postgraduate Courses) | Master's applicants from developing countries (incl. Bangladesh) with a **4-year Bachelor's + ≥ 2 years' relevant work experience**, development-related motivation | **€992/month** (Master's) + health insurance + travel + often tuition; PhD ~€1,400/month from 2026 |
| **DAAD Study Scholarships** | Graduates for a full Master's in Germany | Monthly stipend + insurance + travel |
| **Deutschlandstipendium** | Any field, any nationality, at participating universities; merit + need | **€300/month** (≥ 2 semesters), applied for **at the university** |
| **Erasmus+** | Exchange/mobility within European programs | Mobility grant |
| **Foundation scholarships** | Heinrich Böll, Konrad-Adenauer, Friedrich-Ebert, etc. | Varies |
| **University scholarships** | Program/university-specific merit awards | Varies |

- Apply for **DAAD** through the DAAD portal (**DAAD Bangladesh** office supports local applicants).
- ~18 DAAD scholarships are listed for Bangladeshi students; several fund degree study in Germany.
- **Most public study in Germany is effectively "free"** even without a scholarship — a
  scholarship mainly covers **living costs** (and replaces/reduces the blocked-account burden).
